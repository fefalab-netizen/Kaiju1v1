Mission briefing & controls

Join the same room on two devices. The kaiju wins by destroying 60% of the city or all three critical facilities. The defender wins by defeating the kaiju or completing evacuation.

Kaiju: WASD move, drag to look, click smash, R grab/throw, Q stomp, E breath, B block. In VR: grip to grab, release to throw; trigger smash; B/Y stomp; A/X breath; right-stick click blocks.

Commander: choose a unit or ability, tap the map to preview, then confirm. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. Select a unit to give orders. Desktop shortcuts: 1–3 units, 4–6 abilities, V select, Escape cancel.
